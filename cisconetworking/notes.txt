Communication Protocols
 Communication in our daily lives takes many forms and occurs in many environments. We have different 
expectations depending on whether we are chatting via the internet or participating in a job interview.
Each situation has its corresponding expected behaviors and styles.

 Before beginning to communicate with each other, we establish rules or aggrements to govern the
converstions. These aggrements include the following.

 * What method of communication should we use?
 * What language should we use?
 * Do we need to confirm that our messages are received?
